# Fun

Sources:

- MDA: Mechanics, Dynamics, Aesthetics. Hunicke, LeBlanc, Zubek, 2004. Designers make mechanics. Players meet aesthetics. Fun is not a line of code.
- Sid Meier: a game is a series of interesting decisions.
- https://github.com/saschb2b/skills — MIT. `skills/productivity/game-design` starts from the experience and refuses to code "fun" directly.
- https://github.com/qiuaoru-coder/game-design-agent-skills — MIT. `game-design-reality-check`: a coherent document is not evidence the game is fun.

Rule. Decide the experience first. Build the smallest mechanic that can produce it. A written design is a hypothesis. Evidence is a played session, then a score another later action actually reads. If the score cannot move without breaking a lock, stop on that beat.

The five gates used on a played beat stay threat, tell, commit, answer, and remainder. This card does not replace that score. It stops a new essay from standing in for a play. The round that has to exist before that score is [experience loop](experience-loop.md).

Leave. Do not treat star count on a design-skill repo as proof. The 19-star and 1-star repos above are packaging. MDA and a played trace are the sources.
