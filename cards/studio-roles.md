# Studio roles

Source: https://github.com/Donchitos/Claude-Code-Game-Studios — MIT. The page https://www.mdskills.ai/zh/skills/claude-code-game-studios is a catalog of that repo. Read 2026-10-13. The art roster is `art-director` and `technical-artist`. There is no concept artist.

Rule. A studio role writes the decision it owns, then stops. The art director writes the art bible, the palette, and the asset spec, and is forbidden to create the pixel or 3D art. The asset spec may include a prompt for another image tool. A picture exists only after a person accepts that spec and a later step draws it. The technical artist writes shaders and checks budgets. They do not paint the concept.

Leave. Do not clone the agent pack into this repo. Do not treat a written art bible as the concept art. Do not open that pipeline on a beat that has no acceptance scene. The round is still [experience loop](experience-loop.md).
