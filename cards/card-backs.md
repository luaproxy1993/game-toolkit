# Card backs

Sources:

- https://github.com/htdebeer/SVG-cards — LGPL-2.1, ~181 stars. Vector poker faces plus a tintable `back`. Bicycle-style, not a campaign look.
- https://github.com/AustinGabriel/Public-Domain-and-CC0-Playing-Cards — CC0. Full 54-card PNG/SVG plus blue and red backs. Safe to print. Generic.
- https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-card-dark-cosmic — CC0, Cethiel. Dark fantasy card backs and frames. Closest OSS gothic pack; still cosmic purple, not Barovia oil.
- https://opengameart.org/content/vintage-playing-cards — CC0, minime453. Worn print texture. Useful as dirt, not as a crest.

Rule. An OSS card back is a composition reference. It is not the ship file.

Barovia talk cards use a native 2:3 oil plate on the live path (`public/assets/barovia/living-deck/card-back.jpg`). Stretching a Kenney/OGA/SVG-cards back into that slot is unfinished. Generate at 2:3, no lettering, burgundy and tarnished gold, one center mark (castle in mist, oil rose, or skull). Keep the old print rose off the live path (`back-print.jpg`).

Leave. Do not vendor an OSS deck into the campaign. Do not put Chinese or English on the reverse.
