# Dialogue

Sources:

- https://github.com/inkle/ink — MIT, about 5.0k stars, pushed 2026-05-05. The prose language behind 80 Days and Heaven's Vault.
- https://github.com/YarnSpinnerTool/YarnSpinner — MIT, about 2.9k stars, pushed 2026-10-01. Lines, options, and commands. Night in the Woods, A Short Hike, DREDGE.
- https://github.com/gamedev-skills/awesome-gamedev-agent-skills — Apache-2.0, about 1.5k stars, pushed 2026-10-09. `skills/disciplines/dialogue-systems` states the choice.

Rule. Pick one authoring shape and stop.

- Ink when a writer needs weave and gather, nested branches in prose.
- Yarn Spinner when a line must fire an engine command and the writer still works in nodes.
- A small graph plus a runner when the game already has actions, flags, and conditions. Build the graph. Do not invent a language.

The English Barovia pack is the third shape. Do not migrate it to Ink or Yarn unless a beat needs flow the graph cannot say.

Leave. Do not vendor either compiler. Do not add a second dialogue syntax beside the pack tables.
