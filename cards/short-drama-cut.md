# Short-drama cut

Source: https://github.com/YvonneMovingon/short-drama-skills — MIT. Shot-splitting rules in `skills/01-通用叙事拆解/prompt.md`. The director sheet at https://github.com/UncleWang2088/short-drama-director has no stated license, so it is not the card. Do not vendor either repo.

Rule. Cut a spoken scene only when the next picture adds something.

- A new shot needs new information: a turn in the line, a prop the line is about, or the listener. Do not cut for rhythm alone.
- A line longer than about eight seconds cannot stay on one talking face. Keep the words whole. Put the rest on a reaction or on the object. The talking face stays under sixty percent of that line.
- The listener is often the shot, not the speaker. When someone is named, show the person who hears the name.
- Emotion that spikes goes closer. A standoff stays locked off. A camera move needs a reason.
- One shot, one action, from a visible start to a visible end. Do not ask a clip to change the face, the place, and the plot.
- Empty views stay under a fifth of the beat.

The English funeral keeps its own lines, flags, and the one ending. This card does not restate them. Do not move that scene onto a short-drama script format or a visual-novel stage block.
